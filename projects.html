Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon (BAH) 2026
Structure-Preserving Enhancement & Pseudo-Colorization of Infrared Satellite Images · 2026
  • Co-conceptualized a hybrid pipeline integrating classical image processing techniques (OpenCV: CLAHE, denoising, edge enhancement) with a proposed Pix2Pix Conditional GAN framework for structure-preserving pseudo-colorization of monochromatic infrared satellite imagery.
  • Designed the conceptual workflow targeting applications in disaster management, border surveillance, environmental monitoring, precision agriculture, and search-and-rescue operations.
  • Collaborated with a four-member interdisciplinary team (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) to develop the technical concept note, methodology, and hackathon submission.
